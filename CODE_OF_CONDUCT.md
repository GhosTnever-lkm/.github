# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this community to be open, respectful, and constructive for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Stay on topic; keep discussions technical and evidence-based.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or bad-faith arguing.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block users who violate
these rules. Report problems through the repository's issue tracker or the
security contact listed in SECURITY.md.
